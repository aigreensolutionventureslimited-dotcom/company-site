Corporate Profile

Global Standard Agro-Logistics Operations

Founded on a commitment to excellence, A.I Green Solutions Ventures Limited has grown into a premier, fully integrated international exporter of top-grade agricultural food products.

Headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, with extensive sourcing networks across West and East Africa, we successfully bridge the gap between local agricultural wealth and demanding global industries across Europe, Asia, the Americas, and the Middle East.

Our vertically integrated model—extending from direct smallholder cooperative partnerships to rigorous portside handling—ensures unmatched traceability, consistency, and compliance at every stage of the global supply chain.

Our Mission

To connect Africa's agricultural wealth to global markets through unwavering integrity, uncompromised quality, and reliable partnerships.

Our Vision

To stand as the foremost agricultural export enterprise from Africa, recognized universally for supply security and operational excellence.

Logistics & Supply Security

End-to-End Quality Assurance Protocols

We mitigate global trade risks by ensuring rigid operational monitoring from initial agricultural farm harvesting directly to vessel loading docks.

1. Ethical Cooperative Sourcing

Direct buy contracts with monitored smallholder collectives checking chemical inputs and field parameters.

2. Lab-Driven Verification

Strict third-party SGS/Bureau Veritas analysis testing for aflatoxins, pesticide residues, and moisture thresholds.

3. Specialized Processing

Industrial size grading, sorting, and contamination clearing utilizing modern machine configurations.
