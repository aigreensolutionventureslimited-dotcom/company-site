Founded in 2012 and headquartered in Lagos, Nigeria, A.I Green Solutions Ventures Limited has grown from a regional commodity broker into a fully integrated international exporter serving buyers across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and the Americas.

Our vertically integrated model — from verified farm partnerships through ISO-certified processing to port handling — ensures unmatched consistency, traceability, and quality at every step of the supply chain.

Quality Assurance

Farm to Port —
Complete Traceability

Our end-to-end supply chain management ensures every shipment meets the quality and compliance standards expected by buyers worldwide — without exception.

01
Farmer Sourcing

We partner with 2,000+ verified smallholder cooperatives and certified farms across Nigeria. All partners undergo annual audits on pesticide use, harvesting practices, and post-harvest handling.

02
Laboratory Testing

Every incoming batch is tested at our in-house lab and by SGS-accredited third-party labs for moisture, aflatoxin, pesticide residues, and microbial contamination before acceptance.

03
Processing

Commodities are cleaned, sorted, dried, and graded in our ISO 22000-certified facility using optical sorters, fluidized bed dryers, and industrial cleaning lines.

04
Packaging

Food-grade packaging meeting destination-country requirements: jute bags, polypropylene sacks, vacuum-packed tins, IBC drums, and bulk containers with moisture barriers.

05
Export Documentation

Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, SGS Pre-Shipment Inspection, Bills of Lading, Fumigation Certificate, and commercial invoice — all prepared accurately on time.

06
Port & Delivery

Containers stuffed under supervision and sealed before departure. We work with Apapa, Tin Can, and Onne ports with regular sailings to Europe, Asia, and the Americas.
